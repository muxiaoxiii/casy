Request for Invalidation

Case No: 2023-IP-491 | Filed: Oct 12, 2023

1. The Petitioner respectfully submits this request for invalidation of Patent No. ZL201810987654.X, owned by the Respondent, on the grounds that the claims lack novelty and inventive step as required by Article 22 of the Patent Law.

2. The patented technology, which pertains to a method for decentralized ledger synchronization, was substantially disclosed in prior art reference D1 ("Distributed Consensus Mechanisms in P2P Networks", published May 2017).

Para 42

42. Specifically, Claim 1 of the contested patent recites "a synchronization node configured to broadcast transaction headers prior to payload verification." As demonstrated in D1, paragraph [0045], this exact sequence of operations was a known optimization technique in the field well before the priority date. The respondent's argument that the timing of the broadcast constitutes an inventive step is fundamentally flawed, as a person skilled in the art would naturally arrive at this solution to reduce network latency.

43. Furthermore, Reference D2 corroborates that separating header broadcast from payload verification was standard practice in high-throughput environments by late 2016.
